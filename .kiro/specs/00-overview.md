# Project Overview — Dhoya-Mukto Poth (ধোঁয়া-মুক্ত পথ)

## Project Name

**ধোঁয়া-মুক্ত পথ | সম্পূর্ণ যাত্রা**  
(Smoke-Free Path | Complete Journey)

## Mission Statement

প্রতিটি ধূমপায়ীর হাতে ধোঁয়া-মুক্ত জীবনের চাবি পৌঁছে দেওয়া — বিজ্ঞান, আধ্যাত্মিকতা ও প্রশান্তির সমন্বয়ে।

*To deliver the key to a smoke-free life into the hands of every smoker — through the integration of science, spirituality, and peace.*

## Project Type

This is NOT a typical commercial project. This is a **humanitarian effort disguised as a business**. Every decision must reflect dignity, sincerity, and spiritual depth.

## Product Description

A bundle of 3 mobile apps designed to help people quit smoking, sold as a one-time purchase with lifetime access.

### The Three Apps

1. **ধোঁয়া-মুক্ত পথ: পদক্ষেপ** (Steps Edition)
   - 41-step behavioral science journey
   - Available in Bangla + English
   - Systematic, step-by-step approach to quitting

2. **ধোঁয়া-মুক্ত পথ: ৩৬৯** (Manifest Edition)
   - Tesla's 369 method combined with Islamic affirmations
   - Available in Bangla only
   - Focuses on spiritual transformation and thought reprogramming

3. **মুহূর্ত ব্রেথ** (Muhurto Breath)
   - 12 scientific breathing techniques for instant calm
   - Available in Bangla + English
   - Immediate relief tool for cravings

## The Offer

- **Original Price:** ৳963
- **Launch Price:** ৳369 (62% discount)
- **Limited Availability:** First 963 customers only (number tied to brand significance)
- **Delivery Method:** APK files via email
- **Access:** Lifetime use, lifetime updates
- **Architecture:** Offline-first (works without internet)

## Target Market

### Primary Market
- Bangladesh (primary focus)
- Bengali-speaking Muslims worldwide
- Middle East diaspora
- UK Bengali community
- US Bengali community

### Demographics
- Primarily male smokers aged 25-50
- Family members of smokers (wives, children, friends)
- Religiously motivated gift-givers

## Success Definition

Success is measured **NOT by sales volume** but by **lives touched per visitor**.

**If one genuine smoker quits because of this website, it is a complete victory.**

This metric fundamentally changes how we approach:
- Design decisions (dignity over conversion)
- Copy (authentic over manipulative)
- User experience (helpful over pushy)
- Pricing (accessible over profitable)

## Website Purpose

The landing website serves to:
1. Educate visitors about the three-pillar approach to quitting
2. Share the founder's deeply personal story
3. Provide transparent pricing and guarantee information
4. Enable purchase for self or as a gift
5. Deliver APK files securely after payment
6. Support users through installation and usage

## Core Values

1. **Dignity** — Treat every visitor as family
2. **Authenticity** — No fake urgency or manipulation
3. **Spirituality** — Islamic values integrated respectfully
4. **Accessibility** — Works on old phones, slow connections
5. **Trust** — 41-day money-back promise, honored without question

## Project Constraints

- Static website (Cloudflare Pages deployment)
- No server-side database initially
- Orders processed via email workflow
- Mobile-first design priority
- Bengali content is primary, English is secondary
- Must work offline after initial load
- Must be accessible to users with limited technical knowledge

## Success Metrics

While "lives touched" is the primary metric, we will track:
- Conversion rate (self-purchase vs gift)
- Time spent on founder's story section
- FAQ engagement
- Installation guide completion
- 41-day promise claim rate
- Refund reasons (for product improvement)
- Social shares (WhatsApp, Facebook)

## Timeline Expectations

This is a mission-driven project. Quality and authenticity take precedence over speed. Every detail matters because every visitor might be someone's father, husband, or son who needs help.
